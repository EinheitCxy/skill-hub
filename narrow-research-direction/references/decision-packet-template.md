# Direction Decision Packet Template

Copy the following structure into the project deliverable and replace every instructional sentence with evidence-backed content.

```markdown
# Direction Decision Packet

## Primary thesis

- One sentence naming intervention, setting, decision objective, and distinguishing constraint.

## Fallback

- None

## Claim ledger

| Claim | Type | Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Atomic claim | Mechanism | A-level source anchor | Testable |

## Overlap matrix

| Candidate | Mechanism overlap | Objective overlap | Strongest prior work | Judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate | Partial | None | Citation key | Retain |

## Rejected directions

| Direction | Evidence-backed reason | Remaining role | Reconsideration condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alternative | Direct overlap | Baseline | New differentiating evidence |

## Evidence boundary

- Evidence level: A for core mechanism; B for the named frontier lead.
- Search boundary: databases, dates, corpus, and citation-chain depth.
- Unknowns: facts not verified.

## Decision gates

| Gate | Premise | Pass condition | Controls and budget | Failure response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G0 | Required premise | Preregistered result | Matched controls | Stop or narrow |

## Failure responses

- Stop condition: terminate the branch when the central premise fails.
- Fallback condition: activate the fallback only after the named gate fails.

## Next action

- Run the cheapest unresolved evidence or experiment gate.

## Proposed canonical updates

- Name files and exact conclusions to update after approval.
```
